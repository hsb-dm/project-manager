2. What we collect

Account information

What we do not collect

3. Google user data

Connecting Google Drive is optional. When an administrator connects it, the following applies.

  • Scope. We request only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, plus your email address so the connected account can be displayed. The drive.file scope limits the application to files it creates itself. It cannot see, read or list any other file in your Google Drive.
  • What we store. An encrypted refresh token, so uploads can continue without asking anyone to sign in again; the connected account's email address, shown in settings; and for each uploaded file its Drive identifier, link and a small preview image.
  • What we do with it. The token is used for one purpose: uploading files your team attaches, into the Drive folder configured by your administrator, and optionally setting the sharing permission your administrator has chosen.
  • Where the token lives. On the server, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. It is never sent to any browser, never written to a backup in plain form and never shared with a third party.
  • Ownership. Files uploaded through a shared connection are owned by the connected Google account and count against that account's storage.
  • Revoking. An administrator can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can revoke access yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all further uploads immediately. Files already in Drive stay where they are, because they belong to the Google account, not to us.

Limited Use. Our use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, we do not transfer it except as needed to provide the feature you asked for or where the law requires, and we do not allow humans to read it except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or to comply with the law. Google user data is never used to train generalised artificial-intelligence models.

4. Artificial intelligence features

If your administrator enables AI features and supplies a provider key, the text of a prompt — and, where you choose it, selected workspace context or an image you point the tool at — is sent to that provider to produce the result. This is off until an administrator turns it on and accepts the terms. The provider is named in your workspace settings, and its own terms govern what it does with the request.

5. Email

We send email only for product purposes: invitations, password resets and notifications you can control. Delivery may go through the mail provider configured by your administrator.

6. Who else sees your data

We do not sell data and we do not share it for advertising. Data reaches third parties only as processors carrying out a function you have enabled: Google (Drive storage and sign-in), the AI provider configured in your workspace, and the email provider configured in your workspace. Your own team members see the workspace content according to the permissions your administrator sets.

7. Security

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Provider keys and Google refresh tokens are encrypted at rest. Sessions are held in HTTP-only cookies, and backups are encrypted. No system is perfect, but access is limited to what each role needs and security-relevant actions are logged.

8. How long we keep it

Workspace content is kept while your workspace exists, because your team relies on its history. Removing a member ends their access. When a workspace is deleted, its content and any stored credentials are deleted with it. Security logs are kept for a limited period and then rotated away.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Write to {{CONTACT}}. Some requests need your administrator's involvement, because the workspace content belongs to your organisation rather than to us.

10. Children

This is a workplace product and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

11. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, tell workspace administrators.

3. Data pengguna Google

Menghubungkan Google Drive bersifat opsional. Ketika seorang admin menghubungkannya, ketentuan berikut berlaku.

  • Yang kami simpan. Refresh token dalam bentuk terenkripsi, agar unggahan dapat berjalan tanpa meminta siapa pun masuk lagi; alamat surel akun yang terhubung, yang ditampilkan di pengaturan; dan untuk setiap berkas yang diunggah, pengenal Drive-nya, tautannya, serta satu gambar pratinjau kecil.

  • Mencabut akses. Admin dapat memutuskan koneksi kapan saja di Pengaturan, dan Anda sendiri dapat mencabutnya di myaccount.google.com/permissions. Pencabutan langsung menghentikan seluruh unggahan berikutnya. Berkas yang sudah ada di Drive tetap di tempatnya, karena berkas itu milik akun Google, bukan milik kami.
